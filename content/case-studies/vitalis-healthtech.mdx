Vitalis required an ultra-reliable, HIPAA-compliant patient monitoring and tele-consultation platform connecting ICU bedside equipment to remote specialists.

<Callout type="benchmark" title="Security & Compliance">
Operating in a strict zero-trust enclave, every telemetry packet is cryptographically encrypted at rest and in transit (AES-GCM-256) with complete audit trail provenance.
</Callout>

## Clinical Requirements

1. **Strict Data Segregation:** Zero-leakage multi-tenancy ensuring Protected Health Information (PHI) cannot cross organization boundaries.
2. **Real-time Vitals Streaming:** Continuous ECG, SpO2, and pulse telemetry rendered at 60 FPS without UI jank or memory leaks.
3. **Emergency Failover:** Redundant WebRTC signaling clusters capable of instant warm handover if an availability zone drops.

## Delivery Highlights

- Built a custom Next.js clinical dashboard with WebGL canvas rendering for patient vitals graphs.
- Enforced end-to-end encryption with ephemeral key exchanges for all audio/video tele-health rooms.
- Configured continuous compliance monitoring with automated vulnerability scans and immutable audit logging.
